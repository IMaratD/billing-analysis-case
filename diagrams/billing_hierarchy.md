# Billing Hierarchy

```text
Direct Distributor
│
├── Direct Reseller
│   ├── Direct Users
│   └── Affiliate Users
│
└── Whitelabel Reseller
    └── Whitelabel Users


Whitelabel Distributor
│
└── Whitelabel Reseller
    └── Whitelabel Users


Integrated Reseller
│
└── Integrated Users
```

---

## Notes

- Billing configuration could be inherited through partner hierarchy.
- Branding restrictions were inherited across whitelabel and integrated structures.
- Affiliate users used independent billing configuration.
- Partner users had restricted billing visibility compared to direct users.
